Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account?

No. Reps has no account and no servers of its own. Your data stays on your devices, in Apple Health if you allow it, and in your own iCloud if you turn on sync. See the privacy policy.

How do I keep my workouts when I get a new iPhone?

Any of these works:

  • iCloud sync: turn it on in Me → iCloud sync on both devices, signed in to the same Apple Account. Your workouts download on the new iPhone.
  • A backup file: Me → Backup and restore → Make a Backup, then on the new iPhone, Me → Backup and restore → Restore from a Backup.
  • Your iPhone's own backup: moving with an iCloud or computer backup of the whole iPhone brings Reps' data along.

Progress photos and form check videos aren't in a Reps backup file; they move with iCloud sync or your iPhone's own backup.

My history looks empty or something is missing

With iCloud sync on, a new or restored device can take a few minutes to download everything; keep Reps open on Wi-Fi. Reps also keeps a weekly backup in Files → On My iPhone → Reps → Backups, the last five, which you can restore in Me → Backup and restore.

Can I bring my history from another app?

Yes. Me → Import from Strong or Hevy reads the CSV export of those apps, and Reps can import other apps' workouts from Apple Health for your streak. Importing the same file twice adds nothing.

How do I restore Reps Premium on a new device?

Open Me → Reps Premium and tap Restore purchases, signed in with the Apple Account you bought it with. Premium also works on your other devices with that Apple Account.

How do I cancel or change my subscription?

Subscriptions are handled by Apple. Open Me → Reps Premium → Manage subscription, or Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions on your iPhone. Turn it off at least 24 hours before the period ends to avoid the next payment; Premium stays on until then.

Can I get a refund?

Refunds go through Apple, not me: request one in Me → Reps Premium → Request a refund, or at reportaproblem.apple.com.

I have an offer code

Tap Have a code? on the Reps Premium screen, or redeem it in the App Store under your account picture.

Reps doesn't save to or read from Apple Health

Check which data Reps may read and write in Settings → Privacy & Security → Health → Reps, and turn on the types you want. Me → Apple Health in Reps shows what it uses.

The Apple Watch app doesn't show my workout

Install Reps on the Watch from the Watch app on your iPhone (My Watch → Available Apps), and keep the iPhone nearby with Bluetooth on. The Watch shows the workout running on the iPhone; to train with the Watch alone, open Reps on the iPhone once so it can send your templates, then pick one under "On your Watch alone".

How do training buddies work?

In Buddies, invite someone with a link; they need Reps and iCloud. You see each other's streak and the monthly challenge, and with "Train together live" on, each other's workout while you train. Nothing else is shared.

How do I export my data?

Me → Export as CSV makes one row per set, for a spreadsheet. Me → Backup and restore saves a complete backup file.

Which devices does Reps run on?

iPhone and iPad with iOS 18 or later, Macs with Apple silicon, and Apple Watch with watchOS 11 or later. The workout generator reads what you type with Apple Intelligence on devices that support it, and with a simpler reader on others.

How do I delete my data?

Deleting Reps deletes its data on that device. With iCloud sync on, also remove Reps' data in Settings → Apple Account → iCloud. What Reps saved to Apple Health can be deleted in the Health app.

Health note

Exercise guides and training advice in Reps are general information, not a replacement for a coach, physiotherapist or doctor. Talk to your doctor before you start training if you have an injury or a health condition, or are pregnant.
