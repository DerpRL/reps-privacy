Training safely

Strength training and other exercise carry a risk of injury. Reps helps you plan and log your training. They are not medical or other professional advice, they don't know your body, and they don't diagnose, treat or prevent any condition.

Talk to your doctor before you start training or train harder, especially if you have an injury, pain or a health condition, take medication, or are pregnant. Warm up, use a weight you can lift with good form, use safety bars or a spotter for heavy lifts, and stop when something hurts or feels wrong. You decide what you lift and how; follow your coach, physiotherapist or doctor over the app. Heart rate and other readings from your devices aren't medical measurements.
