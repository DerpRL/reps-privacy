Reps: Workout Tracker

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

In short:

  • Reps has no account and no servers of its own. I don't receive your workouts, your body weight, your photos or anything else you log.
  • Your data stays on your devices. It also goes to Apple Health if you allow it, and to your own iCloud account if you turn on iCloud sync.
  • Training buddies see only your name, your streak and your monthly challenge numbers, and your workout while you train live if you turn that on.
  • No ads, no tracking, no analytics of its own and no selling of data.

Who is responsible

Reps is made by Yarne Savaete, an independent developer in Belgium. For questions about this policy or your data, write to yarne.savaete@outlook.com.

What stays on your device

Everything you log in Reps is stored in the app on your iPhone, iPad or Apple Watch. That includes your workouts and sets, templates and programs, goals, places, injuries, readiness check-ins, body weight and measurements, progress photos and form check videos. Progress photos can be locked with Face ID; Reps never sees your face data, only whether unlocking worked. Progress photos and videos are never saved to your photo library.

The camera, the microphone and your voice are handled on the device. Rep counting and form checks use Apple's image recognition on your iPhone; hands-free logging uses on-device speech recognition; the workout generator uses the Apple Intelligence model on your device where it's available. The Apple Watch counts reps from your wrist's movement on the Watch itself.

Like all app data, Reps' data is included in your device's own iCloud or computer backup, if you make those. Reps also keeps a weekly backup in Files → On My iPhone → Reps. When you delete the app, its data on the device is deleted with it.

Reps doesn't use the internet itself

Reps sends nothing to servers of its own or anyone else's. Everything that leaves your device goes through Apple's services below, or through apps you choose to open, like Spotify or SoundCloud when you start a playlist from their link.

Apple services

Some features work through Apple. What Apple does with that data is covered by Apple's privacy policy, not by me.

  • Apple Health, only if you allow it: Reps saves the workouts you finish, with the energy they took and, from the Apple Watch, your heart rate. It reads heart rate variability, resting heart rate, sleep, body weight, the protein and calories a food app logged, workouts from other apps when you import them and, only if you turn it on, your period days. What Reps reads from Apple Health stays on that device and never goes to iCloud.
  • iCloud sync, only if you turn it on: your workouts, templates and settings sync between your own devices through your private iCloud account. Data read from Apple Health is left out. I have no access to your iCloud data.
  • Training buddies, only if you invite someone or accept an invite: through iCloud sharing, the people in your group see the name you choose, your streak and this month's challenge numbers (workouts, minutes or sets). With "Train together live" on, they also see the workout you're doing, set by set, until you finish, and can send you a cheer. Nothing else is shared, and nothing is shared when you leave the group.
  • Reps Premium: subscriptions are bought and managed through the App Store. Apple handles payment; I only get sales reports from Apple that don't say who you are.
  • Apple Watch: the iPhone and the Watch exchange the workout in progress, your templates and the next session directly, over Apple's connection between your own devices.
  • Calendar, Apple Music, Siri, Shortcuts, Focus, widgets and Live Activities: Reps uses them when you do, to put planned workouts in a Reps calendar, play a playlist from your library, answer questions about your training or show the workout on the Lock Screen.
  • Crash reports: if you agreed to share analytics with app developers in your device's settings, Apple may send me anonymous crash reports and usage statistics.

Bite, the food tracker

If Bite is installed on the same iPhone, the two apps share a few files on the device through an App Group: Reps shares its plan for two weeks, recent sessions, goals and main lifts, and reads Bite's diet, recent food, fasts and weight trend. These files never leave the iPhone. Without Bite, nothing is shared.

What you share yourself

When you share a workout image, a template or program link, a backup, a CSV export or a report about an exercise, it goes where you send it: AirDrop, Messages, Mail, Files or another app. Template and program links contain the exercises and sets, not your history. A report about an exercise contains what you wrote, the exercise, the app version, your device's system version and language.

Health information

Reps handles information about your health, like your body weight, heart rate, sleep, injuries and, if you turn it on, your cycle. It stays under your control in the places described above. Reps doesn't use it for advertising, doesn't sell it and doesn't share it with anyone. Exercise guides and training advice in Reps are general information, not medical advice.

Children

Reps is not meant for children under 16 and doesn't knowingly handle their data.

Your rights

Because I don't receive or store your data, there is nothing about you on my side to look up, correct or delete. You can see, change, export (Me → Export as CSV, or a backup) and delete all your data in the app itself. To remove the data from Apple Health or iCloud, use the Health app or your iCloud settings.

If you live in the European Union, you can also file a complaint with your data protection authority; in Belgium that is the Data Protection Authority.

Changes

If Reps starts handling data differently, this page is updated first, with a new date at the top.
